Data Science Networks Plus (DSN+)

UKRI invites proposals to establish interdisciplinary networks connecting computer science, mathematics and applied domains including economics, psychology and energy systems research.

Funding

Network grants of up to £750,000 over three years. Around eight networks will be funded.

Eligibility

Eligibility: UK higher education institutions and approved independent research organisations. Networks must include at least three partner institutions and demonstrate involvement from outside academia.

Submission

This is a rolling call with no fixed deadline; proposals are assessed at quarterly panels until the budget is committed.
