Mid-Scale Research Infrastructure — Climate Observation Networks

The National Science Foundation seeks proposals to design and construct mid-scale infrastructure supporting climate and earth-systems observation. The programme addresses a recognised gap between small instrumentation awards and major facility construction.

Scope

Projects may span atmospheric chemistry, oceanography, materials science for sensor development, and the data engineering required to make observations openly available. Physics and engineering teams are encouraged to partner with domain climate scientists.

Award information

Awards between $4,000,000 and $20,000,000. Approximately six awards expected.

Eligibility

Eligibility: US institutions of higher education and eligible non-profit research organisations. International collaborators may participate as sub-awardees but may not serve as lead PI.

Key dates

Preliminary proposal due 14 Sep 2026. Full proposal by invitation only, closing date March 3, 2027.
