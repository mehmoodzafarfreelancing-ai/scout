CISE Core Research Program (CCRP) — Solicitation 26-512

The National Science Foundation's Directorate for Computer and Information Science and Engineering invites proposals for fundamental research in computer science, engineering and mathematics that advances the theory and practice of computing systems.

Programme description

CCRP supports single-investigator and small-team projects across algorithms, distributed systems, machine learning theory, robotics, and human-centred computing. Proposals that bridge two or more of these areas are explicitly encouraged. Awards are made for three to four years.

Award information

Estimated number of awards: 180. Anticipated funding amount: $95,000,000 in total. Individual awards range from $250,000 to $1,200,000 over the award period.

Eligibility

Eligibility: Institutions of higher education accredited in and having a campus in the United States, and non-profit non-academic organisations. An individual may appear as PI or co-PI on no more than two proposals per annual cycle.

Deadlines

Full proposal deadline: 2026-11-18. Letters of intent are not required.
