By award type

Standard grants, continuing grants, cooperative agreements, fellowships, conference and travel support, and equipment awards. Each solicitation states which types apply. Award sizes vary widely between programmes and are described on the individual solicitation page rather than here.

Before you apply

Read the Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide. Every proposal must be submitted through Research.gov or Grants.gov by an authorised organisational representative. Individual investigators cannot submit directly. Deadlines differ by programme and are listed on each solicitation.

Help

Contact the programme officer named on a solicitation with questions about scope and fit. For submission and system problems, contact the help desk.
