Mental Health Discovery Awards

The Wellcome Trust funds research that transforms understanding of anxiety, depression and psychosis. We support work across neuroscience, psychology and population health that identifies the active ingredients of effective intervention.

Award size

Awards of up to £3 million over five years. Smaller seed awards of £150,000 are available for pilot work.

Eligibility

Eligibility: researchers at any career stage based at an eligible host organisation anywhere in the world, including low- and middle-income countries. Teams must include people with lived experience of mental health difficulty in their design process.

Dates

Preliminary applications close 9 Dec 2026.
