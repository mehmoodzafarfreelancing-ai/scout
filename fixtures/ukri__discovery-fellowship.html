Discovery Fellowships in Biosciences and Medicine

UKRI is offering fellowships supporting outstanding early-career researchers to establish independent programmes in biology, genomics, neuroscience and medicine. The fellowship covers salary, research costs and a contribution to training.

What we fund

Four-year fellowships covering 80% of full economic cost. Typical awards fall between £400,000 and £1.2 million depending on research costs and host institution. Funding may include a named technician and consumables.

Who can apply

Eligibility: applicants must hold a PhD awarded no more than eight years before the closing date, excluding career breaks, and must have a UK host organisation willing to provide space and mentorship. There is no nationality restriction.

How to apply

Applications are made through the Funding Service. The closing date for submissions is 22 Oct 2026 at 16:00 UK time. Interviews will be held in February 2027.
