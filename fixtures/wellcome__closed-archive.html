Climate and Health Award 2024

This scheme is closed and no longer accepting applications. The information below is retained for reference, and for researchers planning ahead for a possible future round.

The award supported research on the health consequences of climate change, with grants up to £2,500,000 over four years. Funded projects examined heat stress in outdoor workers, the spread of vector-borne disease under changing rainfall patterns, and the mental health effects of displacement.

Eligibility

Eligibility: was open to research organisations worldwide, including teams based in low- and middle-income countries. Applicants were required to demonstrate an existing partnership with a public health body.

Outcome

Fourteen awards were made. The closing date was 2024-05-30. A decision on whether the scheme will run again has not yet been announced.
